## 4. Summary

RecruitU is a single application that consolidates the fragmented tasks of a job
search into one place: matching a candidate to job postings with an explainable
fit score, tailoring a resume and cover letter to each posting, tracking every
application through to an outcome, and preparing for interviews (role/company
cheat sheets, likely-question preparation, and configurable mock interviews).

The invention was conceived and first reduced to a working prototype by the
inventor while personally conducting a job search, to solve gaps the inventor
experienced firsthand — principally the absence of a single place to track
applications and to grade a given job against one's own CV, experience, and
skills.

## 5. Description

The working prototype (see Evidence, §7) encoded three core functions:

1. **Find & match** — surface relevant job postings and rate each against the
   candidate's profile with a fit score and stated reasons.
2. **Tailor** — produce a role-specific resume and cover letter from a master CV,
   mirroring a posting's requirements, under an explicit rule never to fabricate
   or inflate experience.
3. **Track** — maintain a running application tracker (company, role, status,
   resume version, contact, next action, fit score, notes) from "found" to
   "offer."

The inventor additionally operated, by hand, further functions intended to be
built into the product: an explainable compatibility score, company/role cheat
sheets, an interview question-and-answer preparation pack, and a voice-based mock
interview runner with configurable parameters (technical vs. behavioral, depth,
difficulty, interviewer style). The intended full product also adds multi-user
accounts with per-user data separation, an advisor/administrator dashboard, and
outcome analytics across a student body.

## 6. Distinguishing aspects (stated honestly)

Individual components resembling parts of this system exist in the market. The
distinguishing aspects of this work are: (a) the **integration** of discovery,
tailoring, tracking, and interview preparation into a single candidate workflow;
(b) an **explainable, non-gatekeeping compatibility score** designed to direct a
job-seeker's effort rather than discourage applications; and (c) an
**institutional framing for career-services use**, including an advisor dashboard
and outcome reporting. This disclosure establishes the inventor's authorship and
timeline of this specific design and implementation; it does not by itself assert
patentability over all prior art.
